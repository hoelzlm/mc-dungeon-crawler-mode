# 03: Safe Room rules and contents

**What to build:** Inside the Safe Room nothing can hurt a Crawler: no damage of any kind, no hostile mobs and no PvP. A light regeneration soothes them. A few chests keep stored items across leaving and re-entering, and a refilling basic-food buffet can be carried out. Food items, refill cooldown and regeneration strength live in data files so they can be tuned after playtesting.

**Blocked by:** 02

**Status:** ready-for-agent
