# Dungeon Crawl Mode

A Minecraft mod (NeoForge) that turns survival into a deadly, televised-feeling dungeon descent in the spirit of the book *Dungeon Crawler Carl*, using original names and text.

## Language

**Apocalypse**:
The moment the vanilla world is sealed off and every **Crawler**, including anyone who joins later, is pulled into the **Crawl**.
_Avoid_: Start, intro, opt-in trigger

**Crawl**:
The whole forced descent through a stack of **Floors**, from the first **Floor** down as far as a **Crawler** survives.
_Avoid_: Run, campaign, dungeon

**Crawler**:
A player participating in the **Crawl**, keeping whatever they owned at the **Apocalypse**.
_Avoid_: Player (when meaning a participant in the **Crawl**), contestant

**Elimination**:
A **Crawler**'s death ending their part in the **Crawl**, after which they spectate.
_Avoid_: Permadeath, game over

**Floor**:
One level of the **Crawl**, generated once as its own dimension of room and corridor templates and shared by every **Crawler** on it.
_Avoid_: Level, stage, map

**Floor Timer**:
The single countdown shared by every **Crawler** on a **Floor**, at whose end the **Floor** collapses and kills everyone still on it. A late arrival simply gets less time.
_Avoid_: Time limit, clock

**Descent Window**:
The final ten minutes of a **Floor Timer**, the only time a **Stairwell** can be used. A **Crawler** who has not gone down by the end dies with the collapse.
_Avoid_: Grace period, open period

**Stairwell**:
A hidden, unguarded exit of a **Floor** that leads down to the next **Floor**. A **Floor** has several, found by exploring, with **Stairwell Aids**, or by chance.
_Avoid_: Portal, stairs, exit

**Faction**:
A group of related monsters on a **Floor**, ranked **Grunt**, **Elite**, **Shaman** and finally one **Boss**, with each rank rarer than the last. Every **Floor** has at least three.
_Avoid_: Mob group, clan, tribe

**Grunt**:
The most common rank of a **Faction**.
_Avoid_: Minion, mob

**Elite**:
A rarer, stronger rank of a **Faction** above the **Grunt**.
_Avoid_: Veteran

**Shaman**:
The rarest non-boss rank of a **Faction**, a magic-using fighter that supports others.
_Avoid_: Captain, mage

**Boss**:
The top rank of a **Faction**, optional and not guarding a **Stairwell**. Defeating it opens a larger area of the **Floor** and may reveal a **Stairwell**.
_Avoid_: Guardian, miniboss

**Stairwell Aid**:
An item that helps find a hidden **Stairwell** without giving its exact position: Seeker's Draught, Echo Chime, Revealing Lantern or Fragment Map.
_Avoid_: Compass, hint item

**System**:
The announcing voice of the **Crawl**, speaking pre-written lines that narrate events, award **Achievements** and hand out **Loot Boxes**.
_In-fiction name_: **The Host** (code and data files say Host).
_Avoid_: AI, narrator, announcer

**Achievement**:
A named feat the **System** recognises and rewards.
_Avoid_: Advancement, quest

**Loot Box**:
A tiered container of rewards handed out by the **System**, kept unopened in a **Crawler**'s inventory and opened only inside the **Safe Room**.
_Avoid_: Chest, crate, drop

**Safe Room**:
A **Crawler**'s own private refuge, the same one across every **Floor** of the **Crawl**, furnished as a cosy old farmhouse with no view of the outside, holding their stored items, a food supply and the only place **Loot Boxes** can be opened. Nothing can hurt a **Crawler** inside, but the **Floor Timer** keeps running and a collapse of the **Floor** they entered from still kills them. A light regeneration soothes them while inside.
_Avoid_: Lobby, hub, bunker

**Safe Room Door**:
A visible entrance to the **Safe Room** found on a **Floor**, at least four per **Floor**, looking like a small farmhouse door in a wooden frame. Every door leads to the same **Safe Room** of the **Crawler** using it, and leaving returns them to the door they came through.
_Avoid_: Portal, entrance

**Floor Map**:
A **Crawler**'s own map of the **Floor** they are on, part of the game's interface and shown as a corner minimap or held open full-screen. It starts empty on every **Floor** and fills in as the **Crawler** explores, with **Safe Room Doors** shown prominently once revealed, and a **Boss** defeat revealing a larger area. It marks monsters in red, **NPCs** in white and other **Crawlers** in blue. It is hidden inside the **Safe Room** and for an eliminated **Crawler**.
_Avoid_: Minimap (for the whole concept), radar, waypoint map

**NPC**:
A non-hostile character of the **Crawl** that a **Crawler** can trade with, get items from, learn from, or keep as a pet. Not yet designed.
_Avoid_: Villager, vendor
