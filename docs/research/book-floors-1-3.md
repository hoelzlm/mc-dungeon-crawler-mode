# Dungeon Crawler Carl (Book 1) - floors 1-3 and general crawl rules

Purpose: design reference for mechanics and themes only. Sources were fan wikis and recaps (fandom pages returned 402/403, so no direct fandom access). Items marked **[UNSURE]** are unconfirmed or conflicting.

## Scope note
- Book 1 (*Dungeon Crawler Carl*) covers **Floors 1 and 2**; it ends as the party reaches Floor 3. Floor 3 content (race/class choice, Over City) is mainly Book 2 (*Carl's Doomsday Scenario*). The Floor 3 section is therefore thin and partly from later-book sources.
- The dungeon ("World Dungeon") has 18 floors; each wraps the globe and is built from crushed Earth matter.

## Floor 1 - "The Tutorial"
- **Theme/setting:** Wide marble corridors lit by green lichen; maze of tunnels, junctions, and safe rooms. Floor roughly Earth-surface sized, but regions are not interconnected.
- **Time limit:** 5 days. At zero the floor collapses/is reclaimed and everyone still on it dies. Reported ~2.55M survivors reached Floor 2.
- **Stairwell:** Iron staircases with a fish-demon motif mark each entrance. Crawlers only need to reach one before the timer ends. Collapse of the old floor is the pressure; the book does not clearly describe a guard, but the Ball of Swine is the boss that blocked Carl's route to the stairs (per recap). **[UNSURE]** whether stairs are formally locked behind a boss or just located beyond it.
- **Typical mobs:** Goblins (L2-3, incl. Goblin Engineers), Goblin Murder Dozers (steam tanks), Bad Llamas (L3, "drug dealer" theme), rats, Scatterer Bugs. Carl accidentally triggers a goblin/llama meth war.
- **Bosses/set pieces:**
  - Hoarder - L7 Neighborhood Boss.
  - Ball of Swine - L15 Borough Boss in a spiral maze arena with movable walls; guards a major junction.
  - Tutorial Guild (Mordecai's safe room: fireplace, shelves, classroom) - unlocks inventory/menus and teaches rules.
  - Taco Bell safe room (NPC Sebastian, L21).
  - Early: Carl's first goblin kill (barefoot stomp, achievement), Murder Dozer fight, Donut eating an Enhanced Pet Biscuit (becomes a talking crawler).
- **Floor rules:** Find a guild/safe room to unlock UI; safe rooms block attacks (a player attack in one was stopped by room rules); pets must be Int < 2 to bond; party leader auto-assigned by highest combined stats.
- Carl's level at end of floor: sources conflict (L7 vs 9+). **[UNSURE]**

## Floor 2 - "The Rev Up" (second training floor)
- **Theme/setting:** White floors, cinderblock walls, dim orange lichen; maze like Floor 1, regions disconnected. Moonshine pyramid scheme ("Rev-Up Toilet-Grade Moonshine", "Be Your Own Boss") run by "Karens", with clurichaun/laminak consultants.
- **Time limit:** Reported 6 days (shortened because fewer survived Floor 1). Only one source (crawlerscookbook); not confirmed elsewhere. **[UNSURE]**
- **Stairwell:** Leads to Floor 3. A storm-shelter safe room with cots/bathrooms sits near it; party camped there before descending. Boss chambers near stairs can lock behind the player (bars drop) during fights. Wiki claim: each floor has half as many stairwells as the one above. **[UNSURE]**
- **Typical mobs:** Brindle Grubs (L2-3, then pupae), Danger Dingoes (L5) with Kobold riders, Brindled Vespas (L8, acid-spitting hornets).
- **Bosses/set pieces:**
  - Krakaren (clone, neighborhood boss) in a lair with a moonshine still and cage of grubs; killing a clone makes Krakaren Prime spawn more; fight injures crawlers via explosion.
  - Rage Elemental (listed L93) - punitive encounter triggered by another crawler; Carl lures it toward the stairwell/safe room and kills it with a MOAB device.
  - Kobold boss chamber; fight yields the pet Mongo (velociraptor) who must bond before collapse.
  - "Ralph the gerbil" incident (Carl crushes it barefoot; dungeon reacts).
  - Prince Maestro's show endangering crawlers for entertainment.
- **Floor rules:** Ratings/show system activates (appearances on the intergalactic shows); Carl gets Enchanted Pedicure Kit (unbreakable feet, bonus barefoot hits).

## Floor 3 - "Race and Class" (mostly Book 2; thin here)
- **Theme/setting:** The Over City: medieval-style villages in monster-filled ruins in/around a dormant-volcano city, about Arizona-sized; first floor where regions connect; day/night cycle (nights spawn tougher mobs).
- **Time limit:** Not found. (One summary says the previous floor collapses ~3h35m after arrival - that is the collapse of Floor 2.) **[UNSURE]**
- **Stairwell:** Not found for this floor.
- **Floor rules:** Choose race (about 80% keep their original race; stat changes, possible transformation) and class (skills/spells/stat modifiers). Options depend on prior actions. Sponsors can start bidding on crawlers.
- Mobs/bosses: not found in my sources (later-book content; Desperado Club etc. is Book 2+).

## General crawl rules
### System announcements
- A System AI narrates in text/voice: announces entrance counts and time limits per floor, collapse countdown timers, level-ups, achievements, new loot boxes, floor events, and sarcastic commentary. Notices pop as UI messages (e.g. "Achievement!").
- Registration gives each crawler a Crawler Number and Name. Viewing audience (alien show, sponsors, ratings) influences rewards and danger.

### Achievements
- Awarded for kills, deeds, or silly/humiliating acts; most grant a loot box. Examples: "Podophilia!" (first kill by stomping barefoot); kill-count and boss-kill achievements; first-time actions; party/pet bonding. **[UNSURE]** exact reward tiers per achievement; sources did not list them.
- Sponsors can supply gear if a crawler is popular.

### Loot boxes
- Obtained mainly through achievements, level milestones, and boss kills; also via sponsors.
- Each box type has six quality tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Legendary, Celestial (Goodreads note from the books). In Book 1 mostly lower tiers appear; Celestial is rare/later. **[UNSURE]** box type names (Adventurer/Hunter etc.) and exact book-1 tier availability.
- Contents: gear, consumables (e.g. Enhanced Pet Biscuit), skills/skill upgrades, special items. Opened from inventory.

### Death
- Permanent for crawlers in practice; floor collapse kills anyone who has not reached a stairwell in time. Other deaths come from mobs, hazards, other crawlers (restricted in safe rooms). Pets must bond before collapse. **[UNSURE]** Any respawn/resurrection is a later-book concept, not Book 1.

### Levels and stats
- Carl enters at Level 1. Five stats: Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, Constitution, Charisma; average human starts 3-5 each.
- XP from kills/achievements gives levels; level-ups grant stat points the player allocates. **[UNSURE]** points per level (I recall 3 but did not confirm from a source).
- Mob level shown by name tags; boss tiers: Neighborhood < Borough < Floor-level.
- Party formation is automatic for those entering together; leader = highest combined stats.
